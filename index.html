STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW

OUR 6-STAGE COMPANY INCORPORATION PROCESS

A structured, predictable pathway designed to deliver your official corporate credentials swiftly.

01
Stage 01 • Strategy Day 1

Choose Your Business Structure

Evaluate and select between Private Limited Company, LLP, or One Person Company (OPC) based on your founding team, external capital roadmap, and liability preferences.

Entity Strategy Consultation Director & Shareholder Blueprint
02
Stage 02 • KYC & Name Day 1–2

Submit Required Information

Provide two proposed company names in order of preference, registered office utility address proof, director PAN, Aadhaar identity documents, and passport photos via our secure client portal.

RUN Name Reservation Filing Automated Document Pre-Vetting
03
Stage 03 • Legal Drafting Day 2–3

Document Preparation & Vetting

Our in-house Company Secretaries (CS) and Chartered Accountants (CA) draft statutory constitutional charters: Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Articles of Association (AoA) with founder covenants.

Custom MoA & AoA Charters Class-3 Digital Signature (DSC)
04
Stage 04 • Digital Submission Day 3–4

Application Submission (SPICe+)

Digital submission of SPICe+ Part B forms, AGILE-PRO-S (GST, EPFO, ESIC, Professional Tax, Bank Account), and Director Identification Number (DIN) applications to the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

MCA V3 Portal Authentication Instant SRN Tracking Generation
05
Stage 05 • Regulatory Review Day 4–5

ROC Government Verification

Official examination by the Central Registration Centre (CRC) and Registrar of Companies (ROC). Our compliance team actively monitors and responds to statutory clarifications immediately.

CRC Manesar Government Clearance 100% Query Handling SLA
06
Stage 06 • Official Completion Day 5–7

Certificate & Post-Incorporation Kit

Receive your government-sealed Certificate of Incorporation (COI), Corporate Identification Number (CIN), Company PAN, TAN, and corporate bank account resolution kit directly in your dashboard vault.

Official COI with MCA QR Code PAN, TAN & Bank Board Resolution
